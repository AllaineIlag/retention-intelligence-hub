# The Employee's Brief: Exit Process

Your feedback is vital to our evolution. This brief explains how to navigate the exit process.

## 1. Accessing the Form
- **Magic Link**: You will receive an email with a unique link. No password is required. 
- **Persistence**: Your progress is saved automatically. You can close the tab and return later using the same link.

## 2. The Questionnaire
The form consists of 4 main sections:
- **Employee Information**: basic details about your role.
- **Exit Questionnaires**: 7 high-impact questions regarding your experience.
- **Terms & Conditions**: Privacy and data usage acknowledgement.
- **Review & Submit**: A final summary of your responses.

## 3. The Lock Protocol
**IMPORTANT**: Once your exit interview is scheduled, you will have until **24 hours before the meeting** to make edits. After this point, the form will become read-only to allow the interviewer to prepare.

## 4. Privacy & Anonymity
Your responses are initially tied to your profile to facilitate the interview. However, after the internal "Final Seal" process, PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is masked in general turnover reports. Only HR Leads maintain access to full attribution for historical record-keeping.
