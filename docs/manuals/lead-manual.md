# The Lead's Handbook: Strategic Command

Welcome, Commander. This manual outlines your strategic capabilities within the Retention Intelligence Hub.

## 1. The Barracks (User Management)
Navigate to **Team Management** to control your personnel:
- **Inviting Interviewers**: Enter an email to send a Magic Link invite. New accounts remain in "Invited" status until the first login.
- **Permission Control**: Toggle `Can Export Data` for specific interviewers to restrict or permit data downloads.
- **System Discipline**: You have the authority to suspend or reactivate accounts as needed.

## 2. Intelligence Deep-Dive (Analytics)
Your dashboard provides real-time situational awareness:
- **Retention Rate**: The percentage of employees successfully retained.
- **Turnover Trends**: Visual representation of exits over time.
- **Top Exit Reasons**: Critical insights derived from Q1 of the exit form.
- **Recommendation Rate**: Measures internal company sentiment (Q7).

### Analytics Filters
Use the `DateRangePicker` and `DepartmentFilter` to isolate specific data cohorts. This allows for granular analysis of turnover "hotspots" within the organization.

## 3. High-Level Approval
As a Lead, you have full visibility into all active resignations and can override states if necessary, though it is recommended to let the specialists (Interviewers) handle the standard flow.
